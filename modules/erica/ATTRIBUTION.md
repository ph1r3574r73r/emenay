emenay includes **software models inspired by** these open schematics (Mixer, Output, Envelope, Dual VCA, and future VCO3 / Polivoks VCF ports). The models are not PCB Gerbers or verbatim schematic copies; they are circuit-faithful DSP / netlists for simulation.

**Panel artwork:** front-panel Fab/silkscreen renders from the DIY zips are used in the UI (`apps/web-ui/public/panels/erica/`). See also Free Modular faceplate SVGs in `apps/web-ui/public/panels/fm/` ([ATTRIBUTION](../../apps/web-ui/public/panels/ATTRIBUTION.md)).
